LEARNING WORDS IN CONTEXT
A short guide to useful vocabulary practice

A word is easier to remember when it belongs to a situation.
Instead of memorizing a definition alone, connect the word with
an example you can picture.

1. READ THE WHOLE SENTENCE
Look for clues before and after an unfamiliar word.

2. MAKE A CONNECTION
Think of a related word or an experience from everyday life.

3. SAY IT IN YOUR OWN WORDS
Explain the meaning simply, without repeating the definition.

4. TRY A NEW SENTENCE
Use the word in a different situation to check your understanding.

AN EXAMPLE: EPHEMERAL

Meaning: lasting for a very short time.

Context: The rainbow was ephemeral; within minutes it disappeared.

Your own words: Something that does not stay for long.

Another example: The flowers bring an ephemeral burst of color.

QUESTIONS TO CONSIDER
How is ephemeral different from temporary?
Can something brief still be important?
